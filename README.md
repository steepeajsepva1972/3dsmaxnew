# 🧱 3ds Max - Professional 3D Modeling 
**Professional 3D Modeling, Animation, and Rendering Software**

**3ds Max** is one of the industry’s leading tools for 3D modeling, animation, and rendering.  
Widely used in architecture, game development, and visual effects, it provides artists with a powerful and intuitive environment for building detailed 3D worlds.

## ✨ Features
- **Comprehensive 3D Modeling Tools** – polygon, spline, and NURBS modeling  
- **Advanced Rendering Engines** – Arnold, Redshift, and third-party support  
- **Powerful Animation System** – character rigging, motion paths, and simulation tools  
- **Geometry Nodes & Modifiers** – flexible, procedural modeling workflow  
- **Integration with Autodesk Ecosystem** – Revit, Fusion 360, and Maya  
- **Formats:** VST / FBX / OBJ / Alembic / USD / STL  

## 🧠 Why 3ds Max
3ds Max is trusted by professionals across design, architecture, and VFX for its balance between precision and creative freedom.  
Its modeling tools are ideal for high-quality assets, interiors, and cinematic scenes.

## 💻 Requirements
- **OS:** Windows 10 or later  
- **CPU:** 64-bit multi-core processor  
- **RAM:** 8 GB (16 GB recommended)  
- **GPU:** DirectX 12 compatible with 4 GB VRAM minimum
